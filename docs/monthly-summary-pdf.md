# Exporting a monthly summary as a PDF

## What it does

On the **Summary** tab there's an **Export PDF** button. Pick a month with the
‹ › arrows, click **Export PDF**, and your browser's normal print box opens
showing a clean one- or two-page document. Choose **"Save as PDF"** as the
destination and save the file.

## What's in the PDF

- The month and the date you generated it
- A short paragraph summing up the month — how much you spent, which categories
  it mostly went to, your single biggest expense, and whether that's more or
  less than the month before
- A table of total spending per category, with a Total row
- A list of every expense that month: date, category, amount, and note

## Good to know

- **Your data stays put.** This uses your browser's own print/save feature.
  Nothing is uploaded anywhere and no new service is involved.
- **The summary paragraph is written from your numbers**, not by AI — so it's
  instant, always works, and reads the same every time for the same month.
- **Works everywhere** — the live site and a local copy behave identically.
- The button is greyed out for a month with no expenses.
- It's the print dialog, not a one-click download. "Save as PDF" is a standard
  option in that dialog on Windows, Mac, and phones.
